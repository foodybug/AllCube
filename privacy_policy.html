Privacy Policy for AllCube

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

Welcome to AllCube. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you play our mobile application AllCube.

1. Information We Collect

When you play AllCube, we may collect certain information automatically to provide smooth gameplay, high scores ranking, and advertising services:

  • Device Identifiers: We collect unique device identification numbers (such as SystemInfo.deviceUniqueIdentifier and Android Advertising ID) for non-personalized leaderboard ranking submission and advertising frequency capping.
  • Gameplay Data: High score data, stage progress, combo metrics, and gameplay achievements.
  • Device Technical Information: Operating system version, screen resolution, device model, and performance diagnostics.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

AllCube integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify you in compliance with their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: For leaderboard, achievements, and Android ecosystem compatibility. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: For displaying in-game banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may use device advertising IDs to serve ads. AdMob Privacy Info

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To operate and maintain the core gameplay features of AllCube.
  • To maintain global and local high score leaderboards.
  • To deliver and optimize in-game advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • To analyze and resolve bugs, crashes, or technical performance issues.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not store personally identifiable accounts or names on our servers. All leaderboard data is associated with anonymous device identifiers. Users may request data deletion or clear local game saves at any time by uninstalling the application or contacting us.

5. Children's Privacy

AllCube does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such information promptly.

6. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical precautions to safeguard the information processed by our application against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
